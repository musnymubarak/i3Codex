About i3Codex

Engineers who build
for the real world.

Founded in 2017 in San Francisco, i3Codex brings together specialized craftspeople across mobile applications, modern web platforms, DevOps cloud infrastructure, applied AI, smart contracts, and cybersecurity. No account managers — only hands-on builders.

the i3codex team · all engineers, no account managers

Three things we won't bend on

principle / 01

Measure before touching anything.

Every engagement starts with rigorous architectural tracing, user journey profiling, and empirical telemetry before writing code. That disciplined foundation is why our rollouts succeed on schedule.

principle / 02

Build so you can own everything.

Codebases, mobile assets, Terraform scripts, AI training harnesses, and deployment keys live directly in your repositories. Zero vendor lock-in, zero proprietary runtime black boxes.

principle / 03

Say no to work we'd do badly.

We decline roughly 1 in 3 inquiries we receive, usually because off-the-shelf software or specialized niche agencies would serve you better. We will gladly introduce you to them.
