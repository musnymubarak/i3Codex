the brief

Every payday, the same outage.

[Client] ran a monolith on [N] virtual machines that had been scaled by hand since [year]. It held fine on an average Tuesday. On the last Friday of the month — payday for most of their customers — traffic tripled inside an hour and checkout queued until it timed out.

[Two or three more sentences: what they had already tried, what the internal team believed the cause was, and why they brought i3Codex in rather than hiring.]

what we did

We moved the queue, not the monolith.

A full rewrite would have taken a year. Instead we pulled checkout out as its own service behind an event queue, put the rest of the monolith on managed infrastructure unchanged, and made the whole thing autoscale on queue depth rather than CPU.
