DevOps & Cloud Infrastructure Solutions

Typical engagement: 4–8 weeks · From $20,000
what you get — deliverables
Production Kubernetes (EKS/GKE) clusters with autoscaling and GitOps (ArgoCD)
Clean, modular Terraform scripts checked directly into your repository
Automated canary releases, blue-green traffic splits, and instant rollbacks
Comprehensive Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry observability
