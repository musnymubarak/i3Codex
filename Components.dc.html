i3Codex design system — v[1.0]

Components & tokens

Everything on the site is built from what follows. Hand this artboard to whoever writes the CSS — the values here are the source of truth, not the page mockups.

01  /  the two-voice rule
voice — Newsreader
Anything the company says
Headlines, section leads, pull quotes, and numbers that make a claim. Weight 300 for display, 400 for anything under 30px. Tracking stays near normal — never below −0.014em.
evidence — IBM Plex Sans / Mono
Everything it shows
UI, body copy, labels, tables, dashboards. Mono is reserved for genuine technical identifiers — service names, slugs, metrics, code. Never a decorative heading.
The system holds only if this rule holds. A heading set in Plex, or a UI label set in the serif, breaks it — that is the single thing to police in review.

04  /  elevation ladder
e0 — flush
Hairline only. Table rows, inline chips.
e1 — resting
Inputs, secondary buttons.
e2 — card
Proof cards, practice panels.
e3 — raised
Forms, hovered cards.
e4 — hero panel
The product panel that breaks the hero.
Every step pairs a wide soft shadow with a tight dark one, plus a 1px white inset along the top edge. That inset is what makes a white card read as lit from above rather than painted on — do not drop it.

06  /  icons & motion
Icons — 24px grid, 1.6 stroke, round caps
Stroke-based, drawn to a 24px box. Never emoji, never a filled icon next to a stroked one.
Motion tokens
.28s Colour only — links, label states
.32s Buttons — lift and shadow
.40–.50s Cards and panels — larger mass, slower
13–15s Ambient float on 3D objects
easing cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1)
One easing curve for everything. Nothing overshoots, nothing bounces — that belongs to a different direction. Honour prefers-reduced-motion by dropping the ambient float and all transforms.
