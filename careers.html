How we work together

culture / 01

100% Engineers, Zero Middle Management

You work directly with founders and technical leads. There are no project managers assigning Jira points or translating specs. Engineers have direct customer contact and end-to-end architecture ownership.

culture / 02

Paid Pairing Day, Not LeetCode Trivia

We despise inverted binary tree puzzles. Our interview is a paid working day where you pair with our lead on real codebase architecture. We compensate you for your day regardless of outcome.

culture / 03

Deep Work & Asynchronous Default

We protect long, uninterrupted blocks for uninterrupted coding and thinking. Status meetings are replaced with concise async documentation, Loom walkthroughs, and PR reviews.

culture / 04

Remote-First & Globally Distributed

Work from wherever you are most productive. We operate across US, European, and Asian time zones with high autonomy, flexible hours, and biannual team retreats.

Respectful & Transparent

Our 4-step hiring process

1

Application & Review

We review your GitHub, past projects, or written background within 3 business days. No ATS bots discard resumes.

2

Technical Sync (30 min)

A casual chat with one of our practice leads. We discuss hard engineering tradeoffs, past failures, and mutual expectations.

3

Paid Pairing Day

A real-world design & code session on live architecture. We compensate you your standard day rate for your time.

4

Formal Offer

Transparent offer within 48 hours: top-tier compensation, equity grants, home office stipend, and your preferred start date.
